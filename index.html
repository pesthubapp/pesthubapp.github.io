Questions

Is PestHub a subscription?

No. You pay once for setup and we hand it over. The only ongoing cost is your own Cloudflare account, about $5 a month, which Cloudflare bills you directly. AI and texting tools are optional and billed by those services if you turn them on. Technical support after handoff is extra.

What if we need help after handoff?

We can help. Technical support after handoff, like fixing a problem, adding a tool or making changes for you, is charged separately from the setup. Email pesthubapp@gmail.com for a quote.

Does it replace FieldRoutes?

No. FieldRoutes stays your system for scheduling, routes, customer records and billing. PestHub reads from it for your numbers, customer lookups and retention, and writes new door-to-door sales into it.

Do we need FieldRoutes to use it?

No. Pricing, SOPs, the Field Guide, inventory, equipment, traps, vehicles, licenses, time off, policies and the rest work on their own. The FieldRoutes tools switch on when you add your API keys.

Who owns the app and the data?

You do. It runs in your company’s Cloudflare account, the data stays there, and it’s backed up every night. Nothing is shared with us or any other company.

Can we change it after it’s handed over?

Yes. Load your own prices, procedures and policies from the Admin screens, and we teach you how to change wording, add fields or add tools with plain-English requests. No coding.

Do techs need to download an app?

No. PestHub opens from a link and saves to the home screen like an app, on a phone or a shop tablet.

What happens with no signal?

Trap checks, product checkouts and equipment changes are held on the phone and sent when the connection comes back.

Who is responsible for mix rates and safety content?

You are. The label is the law, so review the rates and guide content loaded into your copy before techs use them.

Is the demo real?

Yes. It’s the same app with a made-up company, Demo Pest Co, and made-up FieldRoutes data. Click anything: nothing is sent, and it resets every night. Manager tools are already unlocked; if anything asks, the Management PIN is 224907.
