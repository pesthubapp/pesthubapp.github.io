    Common Questions

    Is PestHub customized for my company?

    Yes. The demo uses a sample company. Your version is customized to your company’s operations and policies, including your pricing, procedures, products, handbook and attendance rules.

    Does PestHub replace my scheduling or CRM software?

    No. PestHub is an internal operations toolkit that works alongside the field service software you already use.

    Do techs need an app store download?

    No. PestHub opens from a link and saves to the home screen like an app, on a phone or a shop tablet.

    What happens when there’s no signal?

    Crawl spaces and rural routes lose signal. Trap checks, product checkouts and equipment changes are held on the phone and sent when the connection comes back.

    Who is responsible for mix rates and safety content?

    You are. The product label is the law, and you review the rates and guide content loaded into your copy before techs use them.
